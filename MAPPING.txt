Statement correspondence and limits

The mathematical target is Conjecture 3 in Raz 2017, also stated as Conjecture 2
in Lu–Raz 2024, not the original union-closed sets conjecture. The public MathDB
page 363118 describes this same auxiliary strengthening.

The statement-facing file uses arbitrary predicates Fin n -> Prop as subsets.
Included means pointwise inclusion. UpwardClosed quantifies over every such
predicate, rather than only a selected list of encoded candidates. Fin m is a
finite enumeration of the member sets; injectivity makes its length the actual
family size and prevents counting duplicates as separate sets. A second
injective enumeration has the same index set, and shared_index_bijective proves
that the common-index pairing is a genuine bijection between the two ranges.

IntervalsDisjoint quantifies over every possible intermediate subset, with its
ordinary four inclusion hypotheses. ReimerCondition combines that condition,
the filter/upward-closure condition, injectivity and containment of each set
in its image. The finite count frequency counts precisely the indices whose
member contains the selected element. An abundant element has 2*frequency >= m.

Nontrivial requires some member to contain an element. Together with m > 0 and
injectivity, this excludes empty-family and all-empty-set degeneracies. The
counterexample supplies this condition explicitly. Its failure of abundance
does not exploit a degenerate universe or a zero-size family.

decode converts bit masks to predicates. every_predicate_represented proves
that every predicate is represented. decode_injective proves extensionality.
condition_iff proves both directions of equivalence between the encoded
condition and the arbitrary-predicate condition. frequency_transfer proves
that their counts agree, including the compatibility of classical and
computable decisions. This prevents a proof only about an unexplained encoding
subclass from being presented as the full set-family counterexample.

Concrete data: n=8, m=11. A_nontrivial, A_injective and A_condition establish the
hypotheses. A_frequencies establishes exactly 5 occurrences for every element.
Because 2*5 < 11, counterexample and not_strengthening refute the universal
nontrivial-family claim. A_not_unionClosed separately checks that the family
is not closed under unions; {8} and {1} are members, but {1,8} is not.
The ground set Fin 8 uses labels 0 through 7, corresponding to paper labels
1 through 8. It is an index relabeling, not a different geometric or infinite
setting. Subset representation, all 11 members and counts are explicit.

The package does not prove minimality of the example, Reimer's average-size
theorem, Lu–Raz's parameterized construction or their closure-count conjecture.
It makes no claim of mathematical novelty, earliest valid formalization or
award entitlement. Its audit uses Lean's normal kernel and standard axioms;
it is not an independent checker or a formal semantic comparison against an
external verifier's separately authored target.
